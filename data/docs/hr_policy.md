# HR Policy Handbook — Northwind Analytics Inc.

## 1. Employment Classification
Northwind Analytics classifies employees as Full-Time (35+ hrs/week), Part-Time (<35 hrs/week),
and Contractor (1099, project-based). Only Full-Time employees are eligible for the benefits
package described in Section 3.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Full-Time employees accrue 1.5 PTO days per month (18 days/year), capped at 30 days banked.
- New hires may not use PTO during their first 60 days of employment.
- Unused PTO up to 5 days rolls over into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is
  forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires payout.
- PTO requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance, except
  for documented medical emergencies.

## 3. Benefits Package
Full-Time employees are eligible for:
- Health, dental, and vision insurance starting on the first day of the month following 30 days
  of employment.
- 401(k) with a 4% company match, vesting over 3 years (1/3 per year).
- $50/month remote-work stipend for employees working hybrid or fully remote.

## 4. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to act with integrity, avoid conflicts of interest, and disclose any
outside employment that could compete with Northwind Analytics' business. Harassment,
discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited and should be reported to
HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

## 5. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without manager pre-approval. Fully remote
arrangements (5 days/week) require written manager and HR approval and are reviewed annually.
Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours (10am–3pm in their local time
zone).

## 6. Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews occur twice a year (June and December). Reviews consider goal
attainment, peer feedback, and manager assessment. Promotion and compensation adjustments are
typically processed following the December review cycle.

## 7. Termination and Offboarding
Employees who resign are asked to provide at least 2 weeks' notice. Involuntary terminations
follow a documented performance-improvement process except in cases of gross misconduct, which
may result in immediate termination. All company equipment must be returned within 5 business
days of the employee's last day.
